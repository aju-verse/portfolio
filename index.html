01 / About

A technical path shaped by a wider view of society.

I am building a foundation in data science and programming while studying the historical, political and economic forces that shape institutions and public life.

What connects these areas is my interest in complex systems: how their parts interact, how decisions create outcomes, and how better tools or evidence can improve the way those systems work.

I learn best through a practical cycle: understand an idea, apply it, test the result, identify weaknesses and improve it. My web projects are one current example of that approach.

Long-term direction

Technology and data for better public governance.

I want to explore how digital systems, data analysis and thoughtful institutional design can support more effective, transparent and responsive public services.
